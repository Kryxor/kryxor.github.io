What is local artificial intelligence?

A local AI model runs on a computer or device that you own — in your office, at your desk or on dedicated hardware. Your documents and queries do not have to leave your environment to reach a model hosted on a remote server. That changes three things: how your data is handled, how continuously you can work, and how much control you keep.

Privacy, security & control

More control, in practical terms

Running AI locally can give you more control over your data and the option to work without sending prompts and documents to external AI services. You can keep selected models on your own infrastructure and configure privacy measures to your needs.

Please note: local operation is not the same as absolute security. No configuration is immune to all risks — we help you make informed choices and reduce real exposures, rather than promise guarantees that no one can honestly make.

Long-term partnership

Support & development over time
